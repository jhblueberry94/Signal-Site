# Pricing, Signal (postsignal.co)

Signal is a LinkedIn content tool for B2B professionals: consultants, fractional executives, founders, agency owners, and executive coaches. One plan, everything included, no tiers and no feature gating.

## Pro: Individual

- Price: £35/month (GBP), $47/month (USD), €41/month (EUR)
- Trial: 7-day free trial, no credit card required to start
- Billing: monthly, cancel anytime, no commitment
- Limits: 1 user
- Features: all three content pillars (Market, Personal, Product), daily research-backed prompts Monday to Friday, unlimited Story Arc post drafts, full Tone of Voice calibration, LinkedIn post preview and one-click copy, weekly Wildcard prompt, 7-day question history blacklist, Monday-Friday streak system, full 9-step onboarding calibration

## Team: Enterprise

- Price: £18/seat/month (GBP), $24/seat/month (USD), €21/seat/month (EUR)
- Billing: billed annually, 5 seat minimum
- Limits: 5+ seats (per-seat pricing)
- Features: everything in Pro for every seat, team leaderboard (streak, output, engagement rankings), admin dashboard with team posting visibility, aggregate streak and engagement tracking, shared topic blacklist to prevent content overlap, onboarding managed by team admin, priority support and onboarding call included, consolidated billing and annual invoicing
- Coming soon: custom pillar configuration per team
- To purchase: contact via https://www.cal.eu/josh-huggins-mkwhu8/30min?user=josh-huggins-mkwhu8&layout=month_view

## Notes

- Currency is selected at checkout; GBP, USD, and EUR are all supported at the rates listed above.
- A limited-time open testing period may offer full Pro access at no cost; standard pricing above applies once that period ends.
